Behavior

  • Search narrows the visible sections as you type and reports the count; an empty result offers a way back.
  • Display name needs at least 2 characters and email needs an at sign with a domain; problems flag inline and focus moves to the first one.
  • Theme picks one of light, dark, or system with arrow-key support. Notification switches toggle independently.
  • Save confirms visibly and survives reload, including private mode; editing again hides the confirmation until the next save.
  • Discard returns the form to the last saved values and says so.
  • Escape clears the search. Enter inside a profile field saves. Enter in the search jumps to the first matching section.
  • Reduced motion is honored: smooth jumps become instant and transitions collapse.
