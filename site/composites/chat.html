Behavior

  • Enter sends, Shift+Enter adds a newline; empty sends are ignored.
  • Send disables for the 400ms local reply window, so double submits append once.
  • The transcript scrolls to the newest message; replies never leave the page.
  • Threads switch locally: click, or focus the list and use Up and Down, Home and End. Press / for the composer, Escape to return to the threads.
  • Include the word “fail” to preview a failed reply, then Retry — focus moves to the retry control and the retry always lands.
  • The meter counts about one token per four characters in the open thread and updates with every sent or retried reply.
