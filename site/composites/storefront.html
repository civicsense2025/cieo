    Behavior

    • Thumbnails swap the main image and alt text; the active view is pressed.
    • Quantity clamps 1–9; the line total is $148 times quantity; the bag accumulates per color.
    • Each bag line carries its own stepper and remove control; stepping down at 1 removes the line.
    • Order total is bag plus delivery ($12 express); the summary recomputes from state on every add, edit, removal, and delivery change.
    • Email and address validate on order with per-field messages; failures use the alert banner with invalid fields marked and focused, and each fix clears its own message.
    • An empty bag cannot order; removing the last line restores the empty state.
    • Every action is a native button or input, so Tab reaches it all; success and error states take focus, and bag changes are announced.
