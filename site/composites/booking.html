Behavior

  • Sundays have no slots — pick another day; Saturdays offer two morning times.
  • Summary and confirm unlock as day, slot, name, and a valid email land.
  • Confirm hides the form, announces success, and moves focus to it; the reference encodes day, time, and initials.
  • Day, time, name, and email each raise their own fix-it note the moment they need attention.
  • A confirmed visit can move to a new day or time, or cancel after a second confirming press; Escape keeps the visit.
  • References read locally: any BK-YYYYMMDD-HHMM code resolves to its day and time, and visits before 10 October 2026 read as past.
  • Keyboard path: Tab moves across days, arrow keys move across times, Enter runs the lookup, Escape steps back from cancelling.
