Foundations

Five ideas, each one you can try.

  • 01Springs that settleButtons, sheets and menus glide to a stop. Nothing wobbles, jumps or gets stuck halfway.
  • 02Motion between screensWhen something changes, motion leads your eye from the old view to the new one.
  • 03Color that stays readableText stays easy to read in every brand, in light and dark, and in high contrast. Checked in 168 combinations of brand, light/dark and contrast.
  • 04Type and shapeOne clean sans typeface, five corner styles and three spacing densities.
  • 05Agents you can trustAI helpers look different from people, and never say something worked before it did.
