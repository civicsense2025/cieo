Foundations that hold across platforms.

Every Cieo control, primitive, and recipe obeys one physical and visual contract: closed-form springs that settle without visible bounce (ζ = 1 default; the 0.7 floor allows at most about 5% overshoot), OKLCH palettes audited across 168 theme combinations, and state machines that never claim an unconfirmed outcome.

  • 01Physics & VelocityClosed-form damped springs (zeta in [0.7, 1.0]), momentum projection, rubberBand bounds, and projectToDetent.
  • 02ChoreographyPresence asymmetry, MotionFrame height morphs, SharedAxis transitions, 2D Edges lag, and MorphSurface FLIP.
  • 03OKLCH Color & ContrastFour-tier semantic tokens and live explorer across all 168 WCAG-audited brand, ground, scheme, and contrast pairs.
  • 04Typography & ShapeEditorial Iowan Old Style display serif, tabular UI sans/mono scale, 5 corner profiles, and 3 density tiers.
  • 05Agent IdentityWhy agents are square with a globe antenna while people stay round, 24 live status glyphs, and non-optimistic approvals.
