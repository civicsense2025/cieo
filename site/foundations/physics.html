Foundations/Physics & Velocity

Closed-form spring physics.

Every motion is evaluated analytically at timestamp t as a sum of damped step responses: zero numerical drift, interruptible mid-flight with continuous velocity, and strictly clamped to 0.7 ≤ zeta ≤ 1.0 so interface chrome never wobbles.

Interactive step response & overshoot explorer

ω = 22 rad/s · zeta = 1.00 · 99.9% settle = 418 ms · Cieo overshoot = 0.0% (unclamped bouncy zeta = 0.45 overshoots by 20.5%)

Gesture boundaries: rubberBand & projectToDetent

Direct manipulation tracks the pointer 1:1 inside valid bounds, compresses asymptotically past limits via rubberBand(offset, dimension, 0.55), and projects release velocity onto sorted sheet detents via projectToDetent(position, velocity, detents).

Detents [0, 0.5, 0.9] → snapped detent: 0.50 · rubberBand(80px, 200px) → 49.4 px
