Foundations/Motion between screens

Change without losing your place.

When a screen changes, people lose their place if everything just snaps. Cieo moves the old view into the new one so the eye can follow: a panel grows to fit its new content, steps slide forward or back, a card opens out of the button that started it. Every one of these motions is short and can be interrupted.

Try four kinds of change

Click a tab or use the left and right arrow keys. The highlight stretches toward the new tab, front edge first, and the panel slides in from the side you are moving toward.

Appear and leave

Menus and dialogs ease in quickly and leave even faster, so the screen never feels slow to get out of your way. Lists arrive one item after another, and the whole cascade never takes longer than a third of a second.

How to use it. Wrap content in the part that matches the change and Cieo does the motion. Give MotionFrame a key that changes with the step, and tell it which way you are going with direction: 1 for forward, -1 for back.

import { MotionFrame } from 'cieo/MotionFrame';

<MotionFrame contentKey={step} direction={step > previous ? 1 : -1}>
  {steps[step]}
</MotionFrame>
