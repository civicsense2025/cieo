Foundations/Choreography

Five choreography primitives.

Coordinated surfaces share a small set of spatial rules: asymmetric enter/exit timing, 60 ms leading-before-trailing edge lag, directional shared-axis steps, interruptible container morphs, and stagger spans strictly capped at 300 ms.

1. Leading & trailing Edges + directional SharedAxis

Click or arrow through the steps below: the leading edge departs immediately while the trailing edge follows 60 ms later, and the content panel slides along the motion axis according to direction: -1 | +1.

Presence & Arrival

Overlays enter on the base spring (ω = 22) from 0.97 scale and exit faster on snap (ω = 34). New list items arrive from ARRIVE = 0.45 opacity, never from 0, and StaggerGroup compresses per-item delays so total stagger never exceeds 300 ms.
