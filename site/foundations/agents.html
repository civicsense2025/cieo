Foundations/Agent Identity

Agents are square; people are round.

A reader must tell in a peripheral glance who acted and who approved. Human avatars are always circular; software agents always occupy a crisp rounded-square silhouette topped with a globe antenna.

1. Silhouette & supervision contract

Software Agent

Square body + globe antenna. Never impersonates a person; pauses visibly on ApprovalRequest.

Human Supervisor

Circular avatar (Avatar). Owns final consent for destructive or external actions.

2. Optical-size status glyphs (24 states)

Every AgentIcon carries three hand-tuned optical tiers (≤20px compact, 24px regular, >24px large) with stroke compensation so outlines stay crisp at badge scale and hero scale alike.

3. Non-optimistic agent pack components

Approval and run controls use Cieo's asyncAction state machine: pressing Approve transitions to pending until the host confirms execution, surfacing uncertain rather than false success on timeout. Inspect AgentStatus, AgentActivity, AgentLanes, ApprovalRequest, RunControls, StreamingMessage, and ToolCallDisclosure.
