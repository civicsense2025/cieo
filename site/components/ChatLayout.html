ChatLayout

Two panes pairing a transcript with an optional artifact rail (chat beside a cited document).

Usage

Your refund is on its way.

Refund policy

Refunds land in 5–10 days.

<ChatLayout rail={<Card title="Refund policy">...</Card>}>
  <ChatMessageList label="Support thread">...</ChatMessageList>
  <ChatComposer onSubmit={send}>...</ChatComposer>
</ChatLayout>

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
default (slot)component—Transcript pane (list plus composer). Required.
rail (slot)component—Artifact panel beside the transcript. Omit for transcript only.

Best practices

Keep the transcript first so narrow screens and readers meet it first.
Don't squeeze two artifacts into the rail; use tabs or a stack instead.
