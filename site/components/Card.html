Components · organism

Card

Section card grouping one topic with an optional title (a billing summary, a profile block).

Usage

Billing summary

$12.00 due May 1 for the Monthly plan.

<Card title="Billing summary">
  <p>$12.00 due May 1 for the Monthly plan.</p>
</Card>

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
titlestring—Card heading. Omit for title-less cards.

Best practices

Group one topic per card with a heading that names it.
Don't wrap a whole page in one card; the boundary stops meaning anything.
