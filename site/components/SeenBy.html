Components/molecule

SeenBy

Tiny overlapping faces of the people who have read a message, with a +n for the rest; a new reader slides in.

Usage

Use the import, not the markup. Copy this line into your code; never paste the preview HTML below.

import { seenByLabel, SeenBy } from 'cieo/SeenBy';
ARBISeen by Ava Reed and Ben Ito ARBICM+2Seen by Ava Reed, Ben Ito and 3 others
<SeenBy people={readers} max={3} />
seenByLabel(["Ava", "Ben", "Cy", "Di"]) // "Seen by Ava, Ben and 2 others"

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
peopleGroupAvatarPerson[]—People who have read, oldest reader first. Nothing renders when empty.
maxnumber3Faces shown before the rest collapse into +n.
classNamestring—Appended to the root classes without replacing library classes.
slotPropsobject—Typed presentation record; see TypeScript declaration. Named parts: root, stack, face, initials.

Guide

Use when

  • Under the last message of a group conversation to show who has seen it.

Avoid when

  • One-to-one chats, where DeliveryStatus says enough.

Anatomy

A span with a stack of 16px faces (photo or initials) and a visually hidden sentence.

Behavior

  • seenByLabel(names): Seen by Ava; Seen by Ava and Ben; Seen by Ava, Ben and 2 others.
  • Faces beyond max (default 3) collapse into a +n tile.
  • When a new reader appears the faces slide to their new places and the newcomer fades in (shared FLIP). Reduced motion skips it.

Keyboard / accessibility

The faces are decorative; the sentence is read. Nothing renders for an empty list.

Do / don't

  • Do: pass readers in the order they read.
  • Don't: pass people who only received the message.

Customization

Face size is set in CSS; colors use accent-soft and sunken tokens.

Limitations

  • A reader who leaves the list slides out of the layout without an exit fade.

Related

GroupAvatar, AvatarGroup, DeliveryStatus.

Host customization

className appends a root class. slotProps exposes root, stack, face, initials. Each part accepts className, style, title, and non-reserved data-* attributes. Existing content props and children remain the composition slots. Library classes are retained; computed inline geometry, state attributes, handlers, refs, roles, and accessibility attributes remain owned by Cieo.

Best practices

Pass real readers only; photos appear only when a src is given.
Don't use it for people who merely received the message; it means seen.
