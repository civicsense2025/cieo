InputGroupText

Short non-interactive addon text inside an InputGroup.

Usage

$
<InputGroup label="Price in dollars">
  <InputGroupText>$</InputGroupText>
  <input type="text" aria-label="Price" />
</InputGroup>

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription

Best practices

Keep addon text to a short symbol or unit like $ or kg.
Don't put inputs, buttons, or long sentences inside an addon.
