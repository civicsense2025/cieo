Components · molecule

ChatMessage

One transcript entry with optional author and time (a reply from Ava at 9:41 AM).

Usage

Ava 9:41 AM
Your refund of $42.18 is on its way.
<ChatMessage author="Ava" time="9:41 AM">
  <ChatMessageBubble>Your refund of $42.18 is on its way.</ChatMessageBubble>
</ChatMessage>

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
authorstring—Message author name.
timestring—Message timestamp.

Best practices

Pair each message with a bubble so entries read as distinct turns.
Don't nest messages inside messages; keep siblings flat in the list.
