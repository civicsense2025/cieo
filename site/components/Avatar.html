Avatar

Single face with photo or initials (one person, at a glance).

Usage

EW
<Avatar name="Earl Warren" initials="EW" />

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
namestring—Full name used as the accessible label.
initialsstring—One to three characters shown when no photo exists.
srcstring—Approved photo URL; omit to render initials.
sizesm | mdmdFace size.

Best practices

Do show initials when no approved photo exists.
Don't guess a photo URL for someone without one.
