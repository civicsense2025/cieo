GroupAvatar

One circle that stands for a whole conversation: one to four faces packed inside it, or three plus a +n tile.

Usage

Use the import, not the markup. Copy this line into your code — never paste the preview HTML below.

import { listNames, groupAvatarLabel, groupAvatarLayout, GroupAvatar } from 'cieo/GroupAvatar';
AR ARBI ARBICM ARBICMDP ARBICM+2
<GroupAvatar people={members} size="md" />
groupAvatarLabel(["Ava", "Ben", "Cy", "Di", "Ed"]) // "Ava, Ben and 2 others"

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
peopleGroupAvatarPerson[]—The people in the conversation, most relevant first.
size'sm' | 'md' | 'lg'mdFootprint of the circle.

Best practices

Put the most relevant people first; they get the visible slots.
Don't guess photos; faces without a src show initials.
