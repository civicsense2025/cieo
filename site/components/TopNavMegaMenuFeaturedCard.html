TopNavMegaMenuFeaturedCard

Featured article card with optional media and link inside a mega menu panel.

Usage

Spring launchThree features worth your click
<TopNavMegaMenuFeaturedCard href="/launch" title="Spring launch" description="Three features worth your click" />

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
titlestring—Card title.
descriptionstring—Supporting text.
hrefstring—Link target; renders an article when absent.

Best practices

Use one featured card per panel next to plain rows.
Don't nest links inside the card.
