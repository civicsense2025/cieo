AvatarGroupOverflow

The +N disc that closes a face stack (how many faces are hidden).

Usage

+3
<AvatarGroupOverflow count={3} />

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
countinteger—Hidden face count rendered as +N.
sizesm | mdmdDisc size; match the stack faces.

Best practices

Do match the disc size to the faces beside it.
Don't render a +0 disc; render nothing instead.
