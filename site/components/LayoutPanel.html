LayoutPanel

Named side panel on the left or right edge (filters rail, details rail).

Usage

Chamber: House. Status: In committee.

<LayoutPanel label="Filters" side="left">
  <p>Chamber: House. Status: In committee.</p>
</LayoutPanel>

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
labelstring—Accessible name for the complementary landmark. Required.
sideleft | rightrightEdge the panel sits on.

Best practices

Name the rail, e.g. label Filters on a left panel of controls.
Don't skip the label; an unnamed aside vanishes from landmark navigation.
