HoverCard

Rich hover card on the Tooltip reveal pattern, wider than a tooltip.

Usage

Serves District 12; sits on Appropriations.
<HoverCard content={<p>Serves District 12; sits on Appropriations.</p>}>
  <button>Rep. Okafor</button>
</HoverCard>

Properties

NameTypeDefaultDescription
contentnode—Card body shown on hover and focus.

Best practices

Keep card content to a glanceable paragraph of plain text.
Don't put links or buttons inside content; hover cards are read-only.
