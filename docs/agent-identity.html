Agent Identity

How agents look across Meridian products: the mark, its states, 24 role icons, 24 action icons and 16 illustrations. Agents are square and people are round. Drawn in the Meridian brand hand: one ink line on paper with a pale wash. Every motion is a critically damped spring.

  • M · Morph one element changes size, radius and color
  • S · Swap content blurs out, then in, never overlapping
  • D · Drag 1:1 with the pointer, settles from release velocity
  • E · Edges leading edge moves first, trailing edge lags 60ms
  • X · Enter/exit popovers, toasts and sheets arrive and leave
  • T · Time loops and counts computed from the clock
  • Success · Warning · Danger end states, from Lamina’s status tokens
